# Source terms and attribution metadata

Every curated release row carries `terms_url`, `license`, and `attribution`.
These fields are release-level metadata for the selected upstream resource and
travel with every parsed observation as release provenance; they are not a
row-level license for every observation in a derived panel. Budget and MYEFO
observation rows additionally carry `observation_source_url` and
`observation_source_sha256`. Rows whose observation source is a PBO workbook
remain subject to the separate PBO terms below even when their enclosing
release-level metadata is a Treasury row.

## Package license and bundled material

`DESCRIPTION` declares `MIT + file LICENSE` for the package's own authored
code, with Chartwell Macro identified as its copyright holder in `LICENSE` and
`Authors@R`. That declaration does not relicense upstream government data,
captured official web pages, or other source material bundled under `inst/`.
The source-specific terms below continue to apply to those materials, and the
package does not claim that normalization, selection, caching, or local
storage expands the rights granted by a publisher.

The package's deterministic test fixtures are small official-shaped replay
inputs generated by the package's development scripts. The scripts describe
them as committed fixtures rather than a redistribution of a full official
archive. A fixture that contains only generated/synthetic values is package
material; any exact extract from an upstream publication remains subject to
that publication's terms. See `inst/COPYRIGHTS` for the component-level map.

## Budget document archive

The archive inventory retains the source URL, referring official Budget index,
retrieval time, media type, SHA-256, terms URL, licence and attribution for
each locally admitted document. It does not assign a licence where the
official resource does not state one, and it preserves corrections and
replacements as separately attributable objects. Archive users must consult
the recorded resource-level terms before reuse; storing a document locally
does not expand its licence or imply publisher endorsement.

The bundled `inst/extdata/ba_idx/*.html` files are captured official index
pages, not package-authored HTML. They reference logos, the Coat of Arms,
fonts, remote scripts, analytics services, and third-party material; the
remote asset bodies are not bundled. The captures also reproduce third-party
loader code inline: all 70 contain a Google tag loader, and 10 contain a
Hotjar/Contentsquare loader. Treasury's Creative Commons grant expressly
excludes third-party content, and no whole-file downstream permission covering
every composite capture is asserted here. Treat each capture as upstream
material and retain its source URL and publisher exceptions.

This file records source metadata; it is not legal advice and does not replace
the terms shown by the publisher for a particular release.
